What Are Image Features & Feature Extraction?

x = φ(I)
Raw Image Matrix I  ——→  Feature Extractor φ(I)  ——→  Feature Vector x = [x₁, x₂, …, xd]

Low-Level Features

  • Individual pixel intensities
  • Regional average brightness
  • RGB color channel histograms

Structural Features

  • Edge orientations ($0^\circ, 45^\circ, 90^\circ$)
  • Closed loop vs. open stroke
  • Horizontal / vertical symmetry

Semantic Features

  • Textures, eyes, wheel spokes
  • Facial geometry landmarks
  • Whole-object parts (learned by CNNs)
Why Extract Features? Good feature extraction amplifies class-separating structure (like loops vs. vertical bars) while suppressing irrelevant variations (like dim paper lighting or minor shifts).

04 • Representation Hierarchy

Feature Vectors & Three Levels of Representation

Core Distinction

1. Raw Pixel Vectors

Unroll every pixel row-by-row directly into a flat 1D array:

8×8 → x ∈ ℝ64
  • Zero manual feature design.
  • Works on tiny centered digits.
  • Fragile: 1-pixel shift breaks pixel alignment!

2. Handcrafted Features

Human-designed math capturing edges, shapes & color histograms:

HOG / SIFT / Symmetry
  • Captures stroke angles & loops.
  • Tolerates small shifts & dimming.
  • Limit: Hard to hand-code for complex 3D scenes.

3. Deep CNN Embeddings

Compact semantic vectors learned automatically by neural networks:

CNN(I) → e ∈ ℝ128..512
  • Encodes high-level visual meaning.
  • Invariant to pose, scale & clutter.
  • Powers modern Face ID & Lens!
Statistical Bridge — PCA: Principal Component Analysis compresses correlated raw pixels (e.g. 64-D → 24-D, keeping 92.6% variance) by dropping uninformative blank border pixels.

Feature Space & Dimensionality

Points in ℝd

1 Image = 1 Coordinate Point

  • 2-D Space: [x₁, x₂] plots on a flat X–Y plane.
  • 3-D Space: [3, 6, 4] sits in a 3D room.
  • 64-D / 512-D Space: Same algebra scales to any dimension d!

Curse of Dimensionality

With millions of raw pixels (d = 106), space grows exponentially sparse and noisy background pixels make all pairwise distances look nearly equal!

Similar images naturally cluster in nearby regions!

Measuring Closeness — Euclidean Distance & Cosine Similarity

Metrics

Euclidean Distance (L₂ Straight-Line Gap)

dEuc(a, b) = √ Σi=1..d (ai − bi)² 
  • Smaller = Closer (0.0 means identical vectors).
  • Squares differences so −2 and +2 never cancel out.
  • Great when absolute intensities & scales match.

Cosine Similarity (Directional Alignment)

simcos(a, b) = cos(θ) = (a · b) / (‖a‖ ‖b‖)
  • Closer to 1.0 = More Similar (θ = 0°).
  • Ignores vector length: dim [3,6,4] & bright [6,12,8] score 1.00!
  • Preferred for HOG gradients & deep CNN embeddings.
Feature Scaling Nuance: When combining multi-unit features (e.g. ink sum 0..1000 + symmetry 0..1), apply StandardScaler (z = (x − μ)/σ) so large ranges don't dominate Euclidean distance!

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) — How Nearby Vectors Decide

Lazy Learner

The 5-Step KNN Classification Pipeline

  1. Store Labeled Vectors: Save training pairs (xi, yi) in memory (no weight matrix training!).
  2. Choose Hyperparameter K: e.g. K = 3 neighbors.
  3. Measure Closeness: Compute distance from xquery to every training vector.
  4. Select Top-K Neighbors: Pick the K smallest distances.
  5. Majority Vote: Predict the most frequent class (or weight votes by 1 / d).
ŷ = argmaxc Σi ∈ NK(x) 𝕀(yi = c)

Why Nearby Representations Work

Without assuming straight-line boundaries, KNN naturally carves out flexible nonlinear decision regions around any visual cluster shape!

Training, Testing & Preventing Data Leakage

Generalization

1. Stratified Split

75% Train (1,347 imgs) stored as neighbors; 25% Test (450 imgs) locked away unseen.

2. Leak-Free Fit

Fit PCA or Scaler only on X_train inside a Scikit-learn Pipeline.

3. Test Accuracy

Fraction of unseen test queries predicted accurately: Correct / Total.

4. Confusion Matrix

10×10 grid exposing exact visual mix-ups (e.g., narrow 8 vs. 1).

Beginner Trap — Why Never Evaluate on Training Images? With K = 1, every stored training image sits at distance 0.0 from itself—giving a fake 100% training score even on pure random noise! Always evaluate on held-out unseen images.

12 • Where Pixels Break

Limitations of Traditional Pixel & Handcrafted Features

Stress Test

Colab Stress Test: +1px Shift & 40% Dimming

Humans read shifted digits effortlessly—yet pixel-by-pixel Euclidean matching collapses:

Raw Pixels (64-D, Clean Test)98.44%
Raw Pixels (+1px Right & Dimmed)52.00% 💥
Aligned + Edge HOG + Cosine (+1px & Dim)98.00% ✓

5 Real-World Challenges Beyond Digits

  • Translation & Rotation: Shifting 2px misaligns grid indices.
  • Scale & 3D Viewpoint: Close-up vs. side-profile object.
  • Illumination Shifts: Shadows & exposure alter RGB values.
  • Background Clutter: Grass/sky dominates 70% of pixels (CIFAR-10 raw pixel KNN ≈ 34%!).
  • Query Latency: Brute-force KNN costs O(N · d) per photo.

Why CNN Embeddings Excel

Sliding convolutional filters + pooling map photos of the same dog—on grass, carpet, bright or dim—to tightly clustered nearby points in ℝ512!

Contrastive & Triplet Training

Models like FaceNet & CLIP explicitly train embeddings so matching concepts maximize Cosine similarity while non-matches push apart.
